Weather that lands on your windows.

Snow piles up on top of your windows. Rain beads on the glass. Autumn leaves settle on the ledges. Drag a window and it all tumbles off. Or let Panefall follow the real weather outside.

Try it here

Drag these windows by their title bars. Flick anywhere in the sky to throw a snowball, drag along a ledge to sweep it, double-click for a snowman.

Weathers

Five kinds of weather, plus the real one.

Each one behaves differently once it reaches your desktop.

Snow

Piles up on top of every window, slumps into soft drifts and slides off in clumps when you move the window. A little snowplow clears the ground.

Rain

Streaks past and splashes on the ledges. Some drops land on the glass of your windows, bead up, merge and trickle down.

Storm

Heavier rain, gusty wind and soft, occasional flashes of lightning. You can turn the flashes off.

Autumn leaves

Leaves tumble down, come to rest along the tops of your windows, and blow away again when the wind picks up.

Blossoms

Pink petals drift across the screen and gather on the ledges. A quiet one for spring.

Live weather

Choose your city and Panefall rains when it rains there, snows when it snows, and leans with the real wind. Dry days show the season.

Play

Reach into the weather.

Your desktop is a little winter world now, and it plays back. Try it in the window up top.

🧤 Play mode

Press ⌃⌥H and your mouse reaches into the weather. Sweep snow off a window, wipe raindrops off the glass, flick snowballs across the screen. esc and you're back to work.

☃️ Snowmen

⌃⌥M and one rolls itself up under your pointer. It waves when you come near, watches your mouse, melts in the rain, and tumbles off if you drag its window away.

🎧 Your music, any app

What's playing on YouTube, Spotify or SoundCloud in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc or Safari, or in Music. Plus cozy rain, wind, thunder and fireplace sounds that never loop.

Control Center

Make it yours.

Click the menu-bar icon and a little panel pops in from the side, with a live weather scene and a snowman who waves back.

  • One tap: weather, Play mode, snowmen, snowballs, gusts, thunder and cozy sounds.
  • Your song: what's playing, with the app it's coming from.
  • Feel: intensity, wind direction and strength, flake size, and how much collects.
  • Where it lands: on windows, on the Dock, or neither.
  • Extras: the snowplow, lightning flashes, and showing over full-screen apps.
  • Live forecast: the next hours for your place, right in the Control Center.

Pause automatically when you unplug, or run at 30 frames per second.

Pause, switch weather or sweep it all away without leaving what you’re doing.

An umbrella for your pointer

Rain and snow part around your mouse and drip off the edges, and clicks still go straight through to your apps.

Every display

Weather falls on all your screens, each with its own piles and puddles.

How it works

Three small parts.

  1. It knows where your windows are

    A tiny native helper asks macOS for the position and size of each window. Only that: no screen recording, no window contents.

  2. It simulates the weather

    Every flake, drop and leaf is simulated. Snow piles slump and spill over the edges, raindrops merge and run, leaves catch the wind.

  3. It draws on a clear layer

    A transparent, click-through layer above your desktop draws it all at 60 frames per second, or 30 to save battery.

Privacy

Nothing to sign up for.

No account and no analytics. The only network request Panefall makes is to Open-Meteo for the weather at the city you choose, and only if you turn on live weather.
